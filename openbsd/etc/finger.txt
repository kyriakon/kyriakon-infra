kyriakon.net

A small, low-extraction hosting platform for the Orthodox Christian
community: mail the platform cannot read, static and Gemini sites, and
pass-compatible git repositories. One box, published configuration, and no
JavaScript anywhere in the stack.

Nobody here has a shell. That is the design rather than a limit applied on
top of it.

Generated from the server configurations on the box, so this is what is

## What this adds

`docs/planning/research/organisation-accounts.md`, the research note for #190: what a
parish-shaped account (own domain, several addresses, one shared site, one bill) needs on
this box and in the onboarding service beyond an individual account.

It works through mail for a second domain, per-address keys, the account model, the service
side and the certificate, and ends with the smallest design that carries one parish, the
operator attention it implies, and what the signup form has to collect. Claims are marked as
read from the box, the source, or a man page, and the unverified list is at the end of the
note.

The box was read only, with writes confined to `/tmp`; nothing was deployed and no account
was created.

Closes #190
